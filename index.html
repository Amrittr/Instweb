THE PHILOSOPHY

We believe a home should hold things that were touched by human hands.

01

Sourced by hand

Clay, glass beads, seashells and dried blooms — every material is chosen slowly, for texture and honesty over mass perfection.

02

Made in small batches

Nothing is mass-produced. Each piece is shaped, painted, and strung in a quiet home studio, carrying a bit of patience and care.

03

Lives in your home

A chime by a window, a diya at a doorway, a resin moon by the bed — objects meant to make ordinary moments feel beautiful.

MEET THE MAKER

Hi, I'm Ankita — and I make things slowly, on purpose.

What began as a way to unwind became a little studio of handmade décor. From beaded wind chimes to glowing resin moons, every piece leaves my hands carrying a bit of patience, colour and care. I take custom orders and love collaborating with brands who value the handmade.
